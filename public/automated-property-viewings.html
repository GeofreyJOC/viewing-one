Automated Property Viewings, End to End

From seller availability to confirmed bookings, see how automated viewings put your diary on autopilot.

What are automated property viewings?

Automated property viewings remove the manual steps between a property being ready to show and a buyer standing in the doorway. Availability is gathered automatically, slots are published automatically, bookings confirm themselves, and reminders and calendar invites go out without anyone lifting a finger.

The result is a viewing diary that runs itself — you spend your time showing properties and closing, not coordinating.

Manual vs automated: the difference

  • Manual: you message the seller for times, wait, message the buyer, wait again, then manually confirm and hope they show up.
  • Automated: the seller marks availability, you publish once, and buyers self-book with confirmations and reminders handled for you.

Across an agency running dozens of viewings a week, that difference is measured in hours — and in viewings that actually happen.

The end-to-end flow

  1. Availability. Your seller marks the times that suit them from a private link.
  2. Publish. You push those slots to your branded booking page in one tap.
  3. Book. Buyers pick a slot themselves; if nothing suits, they send a viewing request instead.
  4. Confirm. Confirmations and calendar invites are sent automatically to buyer and seller.
  5. Manage. Slot capacity is enforced, cancellations handled, and past slots hidden automatically.

Why it matters for estate agents

Buyers expect to book things online instantly. When you are the fastest agent to offer a viewing, you win the relationship. Automation also protects your calendar from being shredded by back-and-forth, and gives sellers a smoother, more professional experience with your agency.

Frequently asked questions

What does 'automated property viewings' actually mean?

It means the scheduling steps — gathering seller availability, publishing slots, taking bookings, sending confirmations, reminders and calendar invites — happen automatically, with no manual coordination from the agent.

Will automation feel impersonal to buyers?

No. Buyers still deal with your agency and your branded booking page; they simply book at a time that suits them instead of waiting for a reply. Confirmations and reminders make it feel more professional, not less.

Can sellers still have a say in when viewings happen?

Yes. Sellers mark their own availability from a private link, so the slots published are the ones that already work for them.

What happens if no published slot suits a buyer?

The buyer can send a viewing request instead, so you can arrange an alternative time without losing the lead.

How quickly can I start?

Your branded booking page is set up in about 30 seconds, and you can add a listing and publish automated slots the same day.
