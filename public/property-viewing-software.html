Property Viewing Software for Estate Agents

Publish viewing slots, let buyers book themselves, and automate confirmations and calendar invites — without the email back-and-forth.

What is property viewing software?

Property viewing software lets estate and letting agents publish a property's available viewing slots and let buyers book themselves — without the email tennis. Instead of you playing diary secretary between the seller, the buyer and your own calendar, the system handles availability, booking, confirmation and reminders automatically.

Viewing.One was built for exactly this problem. Your seller marks when the property is available, you publish it in one tap, and buyers pick a slot that suits them. Confirmations and calendar invites go out on their own, for both the buyer and the seller.

Why agents move off manual scheduling

  • Back-and-forth eats the day. Chasing availability across WhatsApp, email and phone is time you never bill for.
  • No-shows and double-bookings. Manual scheduling makes both easy; automated confirmations and calendar invites make them rare.
  • Slow response loses buyers. Buyers who can book instantly, at 9pm, convert far better than those waiting for a reply in the morning.
  • Seller coordination is the bottleneck. When the seller sets their own availability, you stop being the middleman.

What Viewing.One does

Seller availability links

Send the seller a private link so they can mark the times that work for them. No account needed on their side, and no more phone tag.

One-tap publishing

With availability in hand you publish the viewing slots to your own branded booking page in a single action.

Buyers book themselves

Buyers choose a slot online and get an instant confirmation plus a calendar invite. If no time suits, they can send a viewing request instead, so you never lose the lead.

Slot limits and capacity

Cap how many visitors each slot can take, cancel or remove slots when plans change, and let past slots hide themselves automatically.

Add listings by email or URL

Drop in a listing from your portal by email or URL and Viewing.One pulls in the property details, so publishing a new viewing takes seconds.

Who it is for

Estate agents, letting agents, property developers and managers who run a steady stream of viewings — whether you are a solo agent or a team. It works particularly well for agencies juggling multiple listings and sellers at once.

Frequently asked questions

What is property viewing software?

Property viewing software lets estate agents publish available viewing slots for a property and let buyers book themselves online. It handles availability, booking, confirmations and calendar invites automatically, replacing manual email or phone scheduling.

How is Viewing.One different from a generic booking tool?

Viewing.One is purpose-built for property viewings. It includes seller availability links, per-slot capacity limits, viewing requests when no slot suits, and listing imports by email or URL — none of which generic calendar tools handle well.

Do buyers or sellers need an account?

No. Buyers book directly from your branded page and sellers mark availability from a private link, both without creating an account.

Does Viewing.One send calendar invites automatically?

Yes. When a viewing is booked, confirmations and calendar invites are sent automatically to both the buyer and the relevant parties.

Yes. You can set a visitor limit on each slot, cancel or remove slots when plans change, and past slots are hidden automatically.
